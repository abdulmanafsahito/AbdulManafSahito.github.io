AI Researcher & Engineer  ·  PhD Applicant  ·  Gjøvik, Norway

I'm an AI researcher and engineer specializing in computer vision, medical imaging, and Vision-Language Models (VLMs). I recently completed my MS in Computer Science at Sukkur IBA University (GPA 3.43/4.00), where I ranked #1 in my MS cohort and was awarded the PM's Youth Laptop Scheme 2025.

My MS thesis developed a ROI-guided multimodal framework for automated lesion description generation, benchmarking MedFlamingo, MedLLaVA, MedGemma, and Qwen2-VL across fine-tuning strategies. I completed a fully-funded 6-month research exchange at NTNU, Norway (Jan–May 2025) under the NORPART-CONNECT project with the ISA Research Group under Dr. Ali Shariq Imran — co-authoring an IEEE paper on Swin-B Transformer CXR classification and developing EduRAG, a RAG chatbot under review at Discover Artificial Intelligence (Springer).

I am actively seeking PhD positions in AI, computer vision, or multimodal learning across Europe and beyond. If you're a researcher or supervisor in these areas, I'd love to connect.
